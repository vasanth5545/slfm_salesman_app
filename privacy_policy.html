Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 24, 2026

Welcome to the SLFM Attendance app. We are dedicated to protecting your privacy and ensuring the security of your professional data. This Privacy Policy clarifies our practices regarding the collection, use, and protection of information within our mobile application ("the App"), which is exclusively designed for employees and authorized sales personnel of Sri Lakshmi Furniture Mart (SLFM).

This App is an enterprise tool involving operational tracking and professional authentication. By using the App, you consent to the data practices described in this policy.

PROMINENT DISCLOSURE: Background Location Tracking

"SLFM Attendance app collects location data to enable tracking of your sales route even when the app is closed or not in use."

This data is securely transmitted to your employer's administrative dashboard to monitor duty-hour travel and geofence-verify your presence at authorized showroom locations.

1. Information We Collect & Device Permissions

To facilitate core operational workflows, attendance tracking, and sales management, the App collects the following data and requests specific device permissions:

1.1 Location Data & Foreground Services

  • What we access and collect: High-precision location data (latitude and longitude) in real-time. This includes access to ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, and ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION permissions on your device.
  • Foreground Services (Location & Data Sync): The app uses Android Foreground Services to ensure location tracking and data synchronization remain active and reliable while you are on duty. A persistent notification will always be visible when this service is active, ensuring you are aware of the ongoing tracking.
  • Background Location: We collect location data in the background (when the app is minimized or not in use) to continuously log travel routes during working hours and to verify presence at showroom locations using geofencing.
  • Status Logging: The app actively logs location events, including timestamps of when location services are turned off, when permissions are revoked, or when battery optimization disrupts tracking. This data is visible to administrators to ensure compliance during duty hours.
  • Battery Optimization: To ensure uninterrupted location tracking during duty hours, the app may request permission to ignore battery optimizations (REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS). This ensures the background tracking service is not prematurely killed by the device's battery saver.

1.2 Camera and Biometrics

  • What we collect: Live images captured exclusively through the App's camera.
  • Why we collect it: A mandatory live selfie is required for identity verification during clock-in, clock-out, and re-entry to prevent identity fraud. Images are securely transmitted to the administrative database and never shared with third parties.

1.3 Device and Security Information

  • What we collect: Unique Device Identifiers (Device ID, Model, OS version), Battery status, and Network connectivity logs.
  • Security: We utilize Runtime Application Self-Protection (RASP) to detect rooted devices or emulators to prevent attendance spoofing.

1.4 Phone Call Management

  • Usage: The App utilizes the phone's dialer function (CALL_PHONE permission).
  • Purpose: To allow salesmen to directly contact the showroom office or customers for professional inquiries directly from within the app.

1.5 Push Notifications

  • Usage: Device tokens for Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM).
  • Purpose: To send critical operational alerts, attendance reminders, and management announcements.

2. Data Retention and Deletion

  • Retention: Professional data is retained for the duration of your employment plus a minimum of 7 years for auditing purposes.
  • Account Termination & Data Deletion: Users cannot delete their accounts directly from the app as it is an enterprise tool tied to employment. Data deletion requests must be routed through the SLFM HR/IT Department. Upon termination of employment, your access will be revoked, and data will be archived or deleted in accordance with company policy.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or location data with any third-party advertisers or external entities. All data is strictly used internally by SLFM management for operational oversight.
